Copyright & DMCA policy

Stream respects the intellectual-property rights of creators and rights holders. Stream does not host video files. Episode playback, when available, is supplied through third-party embed providers selected by the site operator. AniList metadata and artwork are displayed for identification and catalogue purposes.

Submitting a notice

If you believe material linked from this site infringes your copyright, send a notice to the site administrator through the contact page. Include your full name and contact details, identification of the copyrighted work, the exact page URL and (if possible) the third-party embed URL, a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized, and a statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.

Review and removal

We will review complete notices promptly and may disable a link, remove associated catalogue material, or request further information. A notice concerning a third-party host may also need to be sent to that provider. Knowingly submitting a materially false claim may have legal consequences.

Counter-notice

If a link was disabled because of a mistake or misidentification, contact the administrator with the relevant page, your contact details, an explanation of the error, and your agreement to the jurisdiction and service requirements applicable to your location. We may forward the notice to the person who submitted the original claim.

This policy does not replace legal advice or any rights available under applicable law.
