Privacy notice

This notice explains how a self-hosted Stream deployment handles information. The site operator is responsible for configuring the service, choosing its hosting and database providers, and responding to privacy requests.

Information you provide

When you create an account, Stream stores your email address and a one-way password hash. Your watchlist, comments, and account creation time are stored to provide the service. Passwords are not stored in plain text. Administrators can review account details and comments for moderation and security.

Cookies and security

After sign-in, Stream stores a short-lived, HTTP-only session cookie containing a signed authentication token. It is used to authenticate requests, is not available to browser scripts, and expires after seven days. The cookie is marked Secure on production deployments using HTTPS. You can sign out to clear it.

Service providers and embeds

Stream uses Turso/libSQL for the configured catalogue database and AniList for anime metadata. If you choose to play a video, your browser connects directly to the selected third-party embed provider; that provider may receive your IP address, browser information, and the page URL under its own privacy practices. Stream itself does not store video files.

Retention and your choices

The site operator retains account data while an account is active or as needed to operate and secure the site. Contact the operator to request access, correction, or deletion. Administrators may remove accounts or comments where appropriate. Applicable local law may provide additional rights.

Contact

For questions about this notice, use the contact page. Your rights and applicable response periods depend on your location and the laws that apply to the site operator.
